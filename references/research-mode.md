# Explicitly authorized research

Research is optional and separate from ordinary acceptance. Record the user's explicit
request, study scope and fixed call/source/time budget before dispatch. Requests to
upgrade or meet the latest ordinary standard do not authorize research.

The existing [evaluation runner](evaluation-runner.md), [behavioral protocol](behavioral-evaluation.md),
[fidelity tests](fidelity-tests.md), [release artifacts](release-evidence.md),
[class-specific scorer](scoring.md) and [register measurements](register-evidence.md)
remain available for a commissioned research protocol. Their transfer tests, named
comparators, register certification and held-out sets are not standard prerequisites.
All live research calls still require workflow reservations. Fix the design before
outputs; do not repeat sampling, replenish sets or loosen criteria until they pass.

Source-grounded package, machine recognition pass, added value over a minimal persona
prompt, likely informed-human acceptance and comprehensive research assessment are
separate claims. Report individual outcomes, original hashes and scope. Historical
results do not certify a changed package. Human review is not mandatory in ordinary
production, and no live comprehensive benchmark is required to validate these tools.

Tool-level calibration needs a separately authorized fixed-budget study. Compare machine
decisions with blinded judgments by people familiar with the source material. Define
the human acceptance question and disagreement handling before labels. Include generic
answers, caricatures, plausible variations and substantive mismatches, not only passes.
An initial product target is at least 80% human acceptance among machine-passed samples.
Report sample size and uncertainty interval, plus acceptable samples rejected or marked
inconclusive. Separate threshold-tuning and evaluation data and report domains/personas.
A changed model or rubric may need recalibration; one subject cannot establish universality.

Position, verbosity and self-preference biases motivate reversed answer order,
source-backed criteria, bounded output lengths and honest recording of judge dependence.
These mitigations do not establish human equivalence. Calibration is not repeated for
every ordinary persona build.
